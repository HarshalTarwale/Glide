# Overview & Business Insights

## Overview (your homepage)

The **Overview** page is the first thing you see after signing in.
Everything on it is live: open sales orders, what's been invoiced this
month, what customers owe you, what you owe suppliers, your weighted CRM
pipeline, and anything below its reorder point. You only see the tiles
that apply to your role — a Warehouse worker, for example, won't see
AR/AP figures they have no reason to act on, but will still see stock and
order information relevant to their job. Nothing is ever hidden by
accident; a tile is missing only because the underlying section (Sales,
Invoicing, Procurement, CRM, or Inventory) isn't something your role has
access to.

## Business Insights

**Business Insights** in the sidebar (under Accounting) is a deeper,
cross-module report for people managing the whole business — visible to
Owners, Administrators, and anyone with a read-only Viewer role. It pulls
together:

- **Revenue, last 6 months** — a simple bar-per-month view built from
  every posted invoice.
- **Top customers** and **Top products**, over the trailing 12 months —
  who's actually driving revenue, and what's selling.
- **AR / AP outstanding** — the same totals as the AR Aging and AP Aging
  reports, side by side, for a quick sense of what's owed both ways.
- **Pipeline (weighted)** — the same forecast number the CRM Pipeline
  board itself computes, so this page never disagrees with it.
- **Low stock** — every product currently below its reorder point,
  company-wide.

Business Insights is intentionally not opened up to every role: it spans
data (AP figures, CRM pipeline, stock levels) that a single functional
role — a Sales Manager, say, or an Accountant — has no everyday need to
see all of at once. If your team wants broader access to this page,
that's a role permission your Administrator can extend.
